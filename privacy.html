Privacy Policy

Effective 19 July 2026

Boss Your Data ("BYD", "we", "us") is a pharmaceutical market-intelligence platform. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the BYD Chrome extension, the BYD iPhone app, the BYD Android app, and bossyourdata.com (together, the "Services").

In short: we collect the details you give us when you create an account, and the queries you run inside the product. We do not track your browsing, we do not read the pages you visit, and we never sell your data.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details — your name, work email address, optional company name, and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in plain text.
  • Requests you send us — for example a demo request for our Pro features, or a colleague's email address when you use the "Invite" feature (used solely to send that one invitation).

Information generated when you use the Services

  • Search and dashboard queries — the terms you search and the companies or substances you build dashboards for, processed to return results.
  • Ask BYD questions — questions you type into our AI assistant, and a daily usage count used to apply fair-use limits.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your sign-in session token, interface preferences (such as the position of the floating launcher icon), and which news stories you have already read. These live in your browser's or phone's local extension storage and are not transmitted to us as analytics.

What we do not collect

  • Your browsing history. The Chrome extension displays a small launcher icon on web pages, but it does not read, record or modify the content of the pages you visit.
  • Advertising identifiers, location data, or contact lists.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and the extension sets no cookies.

3. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers that run our infrastructure, under contracts that restrict their use of it:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and hosting provider. Your account data is stored in Supabase's EU (Ireland) region.
  • Google (Gemini API) — when you use Ask BYD, your question (and the conversation's recent context) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer. Questions are not linked to your identity by Google beyond what is necessary to process the request.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or as part of a corporate transaction such as a merger or acquisition (in which case this policy would continue to apply to your data).

4. Data retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, your profile and personal records are permanently removed from our systems.
  • Local data on your device (session, read-story list, preferences) is removed when you sign out, remove the extension, or delete the app.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account at any time from within the product: open the sign-out menu and choose "Delete account". Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Access, correction, portability, objection — under UK and EU data-protection law you may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or object to processing. Email us at the address below and we will respond within one month.
  • Complaints — you may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

6. Security

All traffic between the Services and our servers is encrypted (TLS). Access to production data is restricted by row-level security policies, so signed-in users can only ever read the data their account tier permits. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing by our authentication provider.

7. Children

The Services are a professional tool intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Not medical, regulatory or legal advice

Boss Your Data is an intelligence and productivity tool. It does not provide medical, regulatory or legal advice, and nothing in the Services should be relied on as such.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be highlighted within the product.
